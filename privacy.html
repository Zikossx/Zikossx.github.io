Privacy policy

WiWi Analytics · last updated 28 September 2026

Who uses it

WiWi Analytics is a private tool. It is used only by the owner of the WiWiPEDIA YouTube channel (@Wiwi_pedia), to read that channel's own numbers. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it can see

With the channel owner's permission, it reads the channel's data through Google's official YouTube APIs, using two read-only permissions:

  • View your YouTube account
  • View YouTube Analytics reports for your YouTube content

It cannot upload, edit or delete anything, and it cannot see revenue.

How the data is used

Only to understand how the channel's videos perform, for example views, watch time, where viewers come from and subscriber changes, so the owner can plan better videos. The owner may use an AI assistant, such as Claude, on their own computer to help read these reports.

Where it is kept

On the channel owner's own computer. The tool runs no server of its own. The sign-in token never leaves the owner's computer, and the reports are not sold, not shared with anyone other than the AI assistant mentioned above, and not used for advertising.

YouTube Analytics reports contain totals and percentages about the channel's audience, not information about individual viewers.

Switching it off

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the files on the owner's computer removes the stored data.

Questions

Contact the WiWiPEDIA channel through its YouTube page.
