WiWi Analytics

A private tool for the WiWiPEDIA YouTube channel

WiWi Analytics reads the WiWiPEDIA channel's own YouTube Analytics, such as views, watch time, where viewers come from and subscriber changes, so the channel can learn what works and make better videos.

It is used only by the owner of the WiWiPEDIA channel, on the owner's own computer. It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.

Its access is read-only. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything, and it cannot see revenue.
